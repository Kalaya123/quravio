How It Began

A Vision for Accessible Luxury

Quravio was born in 2021 from a frustration felt by our founders — that extraordinary products were always locked behind extraordinary price tags. We believed the world deserved better.

"We wanted to build a place where every purchase felt like a discovery — a piece that told a story, built to last, and priced for real people."

Working directly with the world's finest dropshipping suppliers and artisan networks, we curate only the top 1% of luxury goods across fashion, accessories, timepieces, home decor, and fragrance. Every product is personally vetted by our team before it earns a place in our collection.

— The Quravio Founders

Our Journey

The Quravio Story So Far

2021
The Idea is Born

Two friends in Kuala Lumpur, frustrated by overpriced luxury goods, sketch out the blueprint for a better kind of luxury e-commerce. Quravio is conceived on a napkin.

💡
🚀
2022
Launch Day

Quravio.com goes live with just 48 products. Within 3 months, we've shipped to 28 countries and received our first 500 five-star reviews.

2023
Global Expansion

We expand our supplier network to 3 continents, launch a dedicated mobile app, and cross 10,000 customers. Offices open in London and Dubai.

🌍
🏆
2024
Award & Recognition

Named "Best Luxury E-Commerce Experience" by Digital Commerce Awards. We surpass 50,000 customers and launch our exclusive Members programme.

2025
The Next Chapter

New collections, new categories, a refreshed brand identity, and an even more elevated shopping experience. The best of Quravio is still to come.
